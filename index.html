01 / Summary

At a
glance.

I'm Lakshya Pant. A BEng (Hons) Mechanical Engineering graduate from Anglia Ruskin University.

My focus is design-led engineering supported by simulation. I take concepts from CAD modelling through FEA, CFD and fatigue analysis to manufacturable, evidenced designs. I work in AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, ANSYS (Mechanical, Fluent, SpaceClaim, Discovery), MATLAB, Simulink and Qblade.

Outside coursework I'm Treasurer of the ARU STEM Society, a core member of IMechE Railway Challenge Team Heron, and have spent industry days at Ford Dunton, BAE Systems, CNH Industrial, ANB Sensors and Ipeco Limited.

01. Drivetrain Design

Drive System for Railway Vehicles

AutoCAD Drivetrain IMechE Railway Challenge Team Heron

Designed and evaluated four mechanical drive system concepts for our IMechE Railway Challenge prototype train: chain drive, two-stage and three-stage speed reduction gearboxes. Performed torque, ratio and speed calculations for each. The final selected design feeds power transmission, durability and energy efficiency targets into a manufacturable assembly with a documented lubrication strategy.

02. CFD & Thermal

Automotive HVAC Air Duct System

ANSYS Fluent Discovery CFD Simulink

Designed an automotive HVAC air duct system in CAD with optimised flow routing, focusing on airflow distribution, pressure loss reduction and thermal performance. Verified against pressure drop and temperature targets using ANSYS Discovery, Fluent CFD and Simulink. Iterated duct geometry to improve outlet velocity uniformity and split balance.

03. FEA & Optimisation

FEA Simulation for Drone Design

ANSYS Mechanical SpaceClaim Witness Bambu Studio

Optimised a drone design through FEA in ANSYS, SpaceClaim and Discovery to make it lightweight and structurally sound. Ran stress and strain analyses across iterations to maximise the strength to weight ratio, designed an innovative twist and turn locking mechanism for the chassis, and explored additive manufacturing simulations to ensure the design was production ready.
