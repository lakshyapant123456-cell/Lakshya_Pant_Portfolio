01 / Summary

I take concepts from CAD modelling through FEA, CFD & fatigue analysis to manufacturable, evidenced designs. Engineering that is designed, simulated and proven.

My focus is design-led engineering supported by simulation. I work in Autodesk Inventor, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, ANSYS (Mechanical, Fluent, SpaceClaim, Discovery), MATLAB, Simulink and Qblade.

I'm a Student Member of the IMechE and a core member of Team Heron, representing ARU in the IMechE & European Railway Challenge as Mechanical Design Lead. I'm also Treasurer of the ARU STEM Society, and have spent industry days at Ford Dunton, BAE Systems, CNH Industrial, ANB Sensors and Ipeco Limited.

Featured / 01
Drivetrain Design · Team Lead

Drive System for Railway VehiclesIMechE & European Railway Challenge

Mechanical Design Lead for ARU Team Heron's European Railway Challenge prototype train, leading a 6-student mechanical team. Responsible for the drivetrain, brake system, coupling system and final CAD assembly. Featured / 02
CFD & Thermal

Automotive HVAC Air Duct SystemTecosim Collaboration

Designed an automotive HVAC air duct system in CAD with optimised flow routing, focusing on airflow distribution, pressure loss reduction and thermal performance. Verified against pressure drop and temperature targets using ANSYS Discovery, Fluent CFD and Simulink, iterating duct geometry to improve outlet velocity uniformity and split balance.

FEA & Optimisation

FEA Simulation for Drone DesignBAE Systems Collaboration

Optimised a drone design through FEA in ANSYS, SpaceClaim and Discovery to make it lightweight and structurally sound. Ran stress and strain analyses across iterations to maximise the strength to weight ratio, designed an innovative twist and turn locking mechanism for the chassis, and explored additive manufacturing simulations for production readiness.
